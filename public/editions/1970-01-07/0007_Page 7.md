## European Flights

The Great Lakes Colleges Association (GLCA) will sponsor flights to Europe this summer for any GLCA college student, faculty or staff member of a GLCA college or member of their immediate families, leaving New York June 17 for Amsterdam, Holland. The one-way flight to Europe costs $146.50, with the return trip costing $158.50. Return trips will be held at four different times throughout July, August and September.

Interested OWU students must file application in U 16 by Jan. 31. Each application must be accompanied by a $25 deposit made out to OWU with the balance due no latter than Mar. 2. Further information and applications can be obtained in U 16.

## T'script Staff

Transcript editorial staff changes for winter term include the appointment of sophomore Rick Howe as Arts Editor.

Former Arts Editor Cathy Conrad will take over the post of Student Affairs Editor, replacing Bob Palmer, who will serve this term as Community Affairs Editor.

Howe, a journalism and history major from Wilmington, Del., has served previously as a Transcript reporter.
