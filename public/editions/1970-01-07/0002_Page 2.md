## Frosh Plan 'One Act' Plays For February Performance

The OWU speech department will present as the Freshman Play "You Know I Can't Hear You When The Water's Running," by Robert Anderson on Feb. 5, 6, and 7 at 8:15 p.m. in U-7.

The production consists of four one-act plays: "The Shock of Recognition," directed by Jim Schoenberger; "The Footsteps of Doves," directed by Kerry Shanklin; "I'll be Home for Christmas," directed by Roger Drake; and "I'm Herbert," directed by Holly Drake (the former Holly Schatz, OWU '69).

The cast for the first play will be Randy Dayton, Gary Shorts, Richard Eakins, and Vivian Folk; for the second play, Ellen Tobie, Bob Merring, Nancy Goodhue, and Steve Canale; for the third play, Wendy Hamilton, Larry Chiger, and Kathy Fisher; and for the fourth play, Babbie Miller and Mike Wolf. All of the actors are freshmen.

Llewellyn B. Rabby, assistant professor of speech, is the advisor. Scott Chandler is the technical director for the entire production.

## Almstead To Give Recital Next Week

Gordon Almstead, associate professor of music, will give a recital at 8:15 p.m. on Tuesday in Sanborn Hall. Almstead, a bass-baritone by will offer a program of French melodie by Lully, Faure, Dupare, and Tschaikowsky, including a three-part work, "Salust du Bartas" by Honegger. He will also perform German lieder by Brahms, Wolf, and Strauss.

Almstead has a Master of Music degree from Cincinnati Conservatory and has been on the OWU faculty since 1951. Professor of Music Tilden Wells, who also holds a M.M. from Cincinnati Conservatory, will be accompanist.

## Theta Alpha Phi

Theta Alpha Phi, dramatics honorary, recently initiated seven new members, according to secretary Kerry Shanklin. Juniors newly initiated are Bonnie Binns, Jane Riggs and Chuck Levitt. New sophomore members are Debi Bartlett, Rex Butt, Scott Chandler and Doug Kerr.

## Cast Set For Social Satire

"America Hurrah," by Jean-Claude van Itallie, will be presented by the OWU Speech department on Feb. 25, 26, 28 and March 1 in the Quons-hut theater. The production encompasses three one-act plays: "Interview, T.V., and Motel."

The cast is made up of Kerry Shanklin, Al Philpotts, Bill Serow, Doug Kerr, Philip McKenzie, Jo Moore, Bronwyn Hooper, and Debi Bartlett. This cast of eight will play more than 60 individual roles in the play.
