Dance co. opening at Chappelear

The Daniel Lewis Dance Repertory Company opens the spring term Lecture-Artist series at 8:15 p.m. today and tomorrow in the main theater of Chappelear Drama Center.

“The Lewis Company offers a different kind of approach to dance than the other companies presented by the Lecture-Artist series in the past,” Chris Asmussen, series coordinator, said. “The other companies were choreographed by one person. This company offers a wider variety of numbers choreographed by various artists.”

The six dancers will perform four numbers each night and different selections for each performance. They will showcase the work of choreographers Jose Limon, Anna Sokolow and Doris Humphrey, but the highlights of the performances will be those choreographed by Daniel Lewis himself, according to Mary Titus, director of Orchesis Dance Theater.

The company was originally called Contemporary Dance System and was based in New York City. It was created to be a living repository of the works of the greatest modern dance choreographers, past and present. Their performances encompass a variety of modern dance works of the past 30 years.

The Lewis company has been the artist-in-residence for five years at Amherst College, Amherst, Mass., where they hope to become permanent members of the faculty, Asmussen said. Lewis is also director of the dance program for the Lincoln Center Student Program and a member of the Julliard School faculty.

“They have an outstanding reputation, and they have been highly praised for their work in the classroom,” Asmussen said.
