Student senate approves widened library hours

After two readings and a sustained debate over staffing costs, the measure passes on a twelve-to-three vote.

Robert E. Whitman Staff writer Jan 13, 1960 · Page 1

The Student Senate on Tuesday night approved, by a vote of twelve to three, a proposal to extend library hours during final examination weeks, ending a two-month deliberation that had occupied four of the body's last six meetings. Under the measure, the Slocum Library will remain open until 2 a.m. each night of the designated exam period, beginning with the spring term in May.

The debate, which stretched past 11:15 p.m. in Merrick Hall, turned less on the merits of extended hours than on the question of who would staff them. Proponents led by Vice President Margaret Deane '61 cited a petition signed by 683 students.

It is a small thing, but small things accumulate. A library open at one in the morning is a different library than one that closed at ten.
